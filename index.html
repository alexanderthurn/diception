HOW TO

BASICS

OBJECTIVE: Conquer all tiles of the grid.

  • COMBAT: Dice attack dice. Your attack power is the sum of all your dice values. Highest sum wins.
    + + 10
  • WINNING: If you have a higher total than the defender, you win. You take over the territory and move your dice there (leaving 1 behind).
    + + 13
    >
    + 7
  • LOSING: If you have equal or fewer points, you lose. Your dice count drops to 1 and your turn ends immediately.
    + 4
    ≤
    + + 9
  • REINFORCE: At the end of your turn, you get new dice. You get dice based on your biggest connected territories amount. Like if you have 4 territories, but only 3 are adjacent, you get 3 dice.
    +3
  • TURN LIMIT: If no player conquers all territories within 999 turns, the game ends and the player with the most total dice (on the board + in reserve) wins.

GAME SPEED

Controls how fast the game plays. Can be changed anytime in Pause or Settings.

  • Beginner: Full animations & dice rolls. Win chances & input hints shown. Slow bot turns.
  • Normal: Faster animations. Win chances shown. Fast bot turns.
  • Expert: No animations. No win chances. One-click attack shortcut. Instant bot turns.

BOT DIFFICULTY

Choose how challenging the AI opponents are. Set per-bot in the player roster.

  • Easy: Targets bots first. Makes mistakes. Good for learning the game. Player starts first.
  • Medium: Attacks anyone equally. Prefers a dice advantage of 2 or more and plays a bit more aggressive.
  • Hard: Same as medium but plans attacks more strategically: focuses on the strongest player, breaks enemy connected regions to cut their reinforcements, and consolidates its own territory. Player starts last.

MODS

  • Play Mode
    • Classic: Players take turns one at a time. Only the active player can attack.
    • Parallel: Every player (human and bot) can attack at any time simultaneously.
    • Parallel S: Like Parallel, but the currently active player is safe from bot attacks.
  • End Game: What happens when the board is completely filled by a single player:
    • Default: That player wins immediately.
    • Territories: The player with the most territories wins instead.
    • Largest Region: The player with the biggest single connected region wins.
    • Random Tile: A random tile is picked; its owner wins.
    • Max Dice +4: The conquering player's max dice cap is raised by 4 and the game continues.
    • Autoplay: Human players are switched to autoplay and the game finishes automatically.
  • Overflow: What happens when a reinforcement die lands on a territory already at max dice:
    • Classic: The die is redirected to another non-full territory; if all are full it goes to the stack for next turn.
    • No Stack: Redirected to a non-full territory as usual, but excess dice are lost instead of stacking.
    • Full: Reinforcements are placed randomly across all territories. If the chosen territory is full, that die is lost.
    • Reborn: Like No Stack Hard, but if the chosen territory is full it resets to 1 die instead of the die being lost.
  • Attack Rules: How ties are resolved when both sides roll the same total:
    • Classic: Attacker must roll strictly higher to win. Ties go to the defender.
    • Easy Attack: Attacker wins on a tie as well as a higher roll.
    • Tie Die: On a tie, both territories drop to 1 die and no territory changes hands.
  • Attacks: Limits how many attacks a player can make per turn. Unlimited by default.
  • Turn Time: Time limit in seconds for an entire turn. Unlimited by default.
  • Attack Time: Time limit in seconds for each individual attack. Unlimited by default.
  • Max Dice per Territory: The maximum number of dice that can stack on a single tile.
  • Dice Sides: Sides per die. Higher sides increase variance; D1 makes all dice equal.
  • Map Style: Shape of the generated map (Full, Balanced, Islands, etc.).
  • Game Mode: Variant rules for how the map is populated at game start.
